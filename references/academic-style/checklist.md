# Final Checklist

Use the applicable checks before returning rewritten prose. Score and style-card
items apply only when those aids were used; they do not require a scored loop
for a focused edit.

## Meaning

- [ ] The locked facts from step 2 of the focused edit in `SKILL.md` survive
  unchanged, apart from verified, authorized substantive corrections reported
  separately. No unsupported fact, label, citation, or claim was added.
- [ ] The strength of each verb matches the evidence.
- [ ] Any uncertainty in the source remains visible.
- [ ] Each recurring concept keeps the same term; distinct concepts stay distinct.
- [ ] Qualifiers that convey magnitude, frequency, or scope retain their meaning.
- [ ] In-scope technical checks and unresolved gaps are reported accurately;
  a prose edit is not presented as verification of a result.

## Clarity

Check each sentence with the
[clarity guide](https://github.com/fmschulz/stop-slop-skill/blob/main/references/clarity.md). Clarity ranks above
every style item below.

- [ ] The subject of each result sentence is the studied object, its measure,
  or `we`, and the finding or action is the main verb. No action is hidden in
  a noun, and no observed difference became an action of the studied object.
- [ ] A reader in the field can resolve every abbreviation, label, referent
  and number. No working label from a notebook or pipeline remains undefined.
- [ ] Each comparison states its baseline and direction. `This` and `These`
  carry a noun.
- [ ] The result is in the main clause, known information comes first, and the
  subject sits close to its verb.
- [ ] No definition, actor or quantity was supplied by a guess; gaps are
  reported.

## Style

- [ ] Hard flags are resolved or retained with a reason and the residual score.
- [ ] Soft candidates have been checked in context; useful wording remains.
- [ ] Sentence lengths serve the argument and voice; sampled rhythm, if used,
  did not force arbitrary changes.
- [ ] Variation is irregular, not a mechanical short/long/short alternation
  (aperiodicity within band).
- [ ] Information density varies with the content (per-sentence burstiness
  within band). Every sentence still tells the reader something: no framing
  sentence was added to move the metric.
- [ ] Formulaicity is within band: no stretches of predictable filler, but no
  wording strained for the sake of variety.
- [ ] Transitions mark logic: support, contrast, limitation, consequence, or
  scope.
- [ ] Edits preserve links between sentences and paragraphs, including clear
  referents. No connective was added solely for variety.
- [ ] Short sentences that state findings or changes in the argument remain intact.
- [ ] Vocabulary metrics did not prompt substitutions for repeated technical terms.
- [ ] Passive voice is used only where it fits the sentence's job, and no
  passive, hedge, transition or long word was added to move a metric.
- [ ] Punctuation serves clarity; a sampled lean, if used, did not override it.

## Academic Fit

- [ ] The section follows its genre conventions.
- [ ] Applicable [manuscript safeguards](../council-loop.md#manuscript-safeguards)
  were checked within scope.
- [ ] Scholarly patterns were checked in context using the
  [shared guide](https://github.com/fmschulz/stop-slop-skill/blob/main/references/structures.md#scholarly-exposition).
- [ ] Citations remain close to supported claims.
- [ ] The prose is formal but not inflated.
- [ ] The final sentence does not overclaim.
- [ ] The output includes the required caveat when score results are reported.
